# Claim boundary

## Supported by the v3.7 evidence chain

- Detector output alone is insufficient to determine the licensed regime claim; protocol, support scope, persistence, selection, and guards can change the correct interpretation.
- Under a fixed synthetic generator, changing only the observation-compression protocol can move the same structural distinction between hidden, ambiguous/protocol-sensitive, and identified certificate states.
- The recovered 5-world × 4-detector benchmark demonstrates hidden support, phantom surface separation, coarse-resolution non-identifiability, migration-vs-switching distinction, and null false positives.
- In the declared ETF15 market workflow, readout strength, persistence, time-split confirmation, and null-family-sensitive selection status diverge.
- All empirical market results are readout audits with `support_scope = not_asserted`.

## Not supported

This repository does **not** claim:

- a universal or superior regime detector;
- that all physical or statistical states are created by observers or protocols;
- a complete theory of worldhood, feedback-stable states, or cross-protocol gluing;
- generator-supported market regimes or a universal market-regime ontology;
- that an identified market readout is causal, predictive, tradable, or an investment signal;
- universal family-wise inference over every possible measurement protocol or null family;
- that the focused multi-null follow-up replaces the full restricted-lattice audit;
- that universe slices of the same cached ETF panel are independent external replications;
- an exact one-command reproduction of the frozen market headline result from v0.1.

## Interpretation rule

The strongest safe interpretation is:

> RRC is a detector-agnostic audit layer that reports what a detector output is licensed to claim under a stated observation-compression protocol, while keeping readability, generator-support scope, persistence, selection, and guard conditions separate.
